FABRIC & GARMENT CARE GUIDE

260 GSM HEAVYWEIGHT COMBED COTTON LONGEVITY PROTOCOL

THE 260 GSM ADVANTAGE

Engineered to Endure

VELEN essentials are crafted from custom heavyweight 260 GSM combed cotton. To preserve its dense weave, rich color depth, and signature boxy drape, follow our tested garment longevity protocol below.

01

Cold Machine Wash

Wash at max 30°C (86°F) on a gentle cycle with similar colors. Cold water preserves the cotton fibers and prevents yarn tightening.

02

Wash Inside Out

Turn the garment inside out before washing to safeguard the exterior finish, screen prints, and high-density branding from friction.

03

No Chlorine Bleach

Use mild, liquid detergent. Avoid harsh chlorine bleaches or aggressive stain removers that weaken the organic combed fibers.

04

Air Dry in Shade

Reshape while damp and hang or lay flat to dry in a shaded area. Avoid tumble drying, as excessive drum heat causes shrinkage.

05

Reverse Low-Heat Iron

If pressing is needed, iron on low temperature from the inside out. Never apply direct iron heat over prints or labels.

06

Fold, Don't Wire Hang

Because of the substantial 260 GSM fabric weight, store folded on a shelf rather than on thin wire hangers to prevent shoulder stretching.
